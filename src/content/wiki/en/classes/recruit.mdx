## What Recruit actually does

Recruit is a Tier 1 Academy class at 100 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Rusty Sword) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **+20% Melee and Gun Damage**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 Cores | +20% Melee and Gun Damage |
| 2 | Class level requirement | +20% HP |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Scrap sells for +25% Coins |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | +10% Melee and Gun Damage (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Recruit worth 100 Cores?

Recruit is the cheapest possible entry point. If you are deciding what to buy first, this is a low-commitment way to test a playstyle before saving for the expensive tiers.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Recruit show up in multiplayer lobbies: **+10% Melee and Gun Damage**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.
