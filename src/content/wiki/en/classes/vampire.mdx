## Is the Vampire class out?

Yes. Vampire is live and people are unlocking it fast. Its badge, **Unlock the Vampire class**, was created on **7 October 2026** (20:42 UTC).

By 17 October the badge had **30,050 unlocks**, and **5,541** of those came in the past day. Creator videos show the class in play from 10 October, when it came with a class passive rework.

Only the first two rows come straight from the game's data. The tier and price come from creator videos, so treat them as likely, not final.

## How much it costs

Two creators call Vampire a **3-diamond class**. Sicblox Gameplay's 14 October update video calls it the "New 3-Diamond Class". Heatw4ve's 14 October test video calls it a 3-star class.

One video, from Berlian kecil on 11 October, lists the price as **5,000 Cores**. That's the same band as [Tank](/classes/tank/), [Driller](/classes/driller/) and [Gunslinger](/classes/gunslinger/), so it fits. We haven't seen it in a written guide yet, so open the Academy in the lobby and check before you save for it.

## What it does

Nobody has published its passives yet. Heatw4ve said it felt like a 5-star class, even before he'd maxed it out. That's one player's take, not tested numbers.

The class launched alongside a passive rework. Creators covering the same update show a new **Level 5** passive tier and Passive Shards for rolling extra passives. We'll add Vampire's level-by-level kit here once a source writes it down.

## Not the Vampire enemy

Don't mix this up with the **Vampire** enemy. That one has been in the game since March, with its own "Defeat a Vampire" badge. It lives in the Vampire Mansion and drops Golden Keys and Gold Bars.

The [structures and dungeons guide](/guides/structures-and-dungeons/) covers the mansion. The [bestiary](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) has the enemy's stats.

## Should you buy it?

If the 5,000-Core price holds, it's one of the cheaper classes on the roster. That makes it an easy test buy once you own Miner. The [get Cores guide](/guides/get-cores/) covers the fastest ways to earn them, and the [codes page](/codes/all-codes/) has free Cores you can claim today.
