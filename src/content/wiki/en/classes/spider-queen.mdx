## Is Spider Queen out?

Not yet. The badge **Unlock the Spider Queen class** was created on **31 October 2026** (02:59 UTC). It had **zero unlocks** when we checked.

That means the class is in the game's data but players can't get it yet. It's the 32nd class.

## How we know it's coming

The developers create a badge whenever they add a class. A new badge with zero unlocks means the class is staged. The first unlocks mean it's live.

Mercenary went this way: badge on 23 September, live on 26 September. Spider Queen may follow, but nothing says so yet. The [Timekeeper](/classes/timekeeper/) badge from 23 October is staged the same way.
