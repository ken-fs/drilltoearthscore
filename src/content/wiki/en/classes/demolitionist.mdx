## What Demolitionist actually does

Demolitionist costs 10,000 Cores — double the 5,000-Core combat classes — and it is the only class that turns **wall-breaking into ore income**.

That is the whole pitch. Every team breaks walls constantly: Little and Large Walls appear **every 700 m to 1 km**, and Boss Walls sit at the end of each layer. If half of those walls drop ore, you have converted the game's most repetitive activity into a resource stream.

You start each run with an **RPG**, a **Demolitionist Chest** and a **Demolitionist Helmet**.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10,000 Cores | +50% Explosive Damage |
| 2 | Deal 100,000 explosive damage | Walls broken by explosives have a 50% chance to spawn Ores |
| 3 | Deal 500,000 explosive damage | Explosives are 50% off in Settlements |
| 4 | 10,000,000 m career total depth | Team bonus: +25% Explosive Damage for the whole server |

## The 50% ore spawn is the real upgrade

Level 1 is a flat damage bonus, and it is fine. **Level 2 is what you are actually paying 10,000 Cores for.**

Walls appear every 700 m to 1 km. Over a 100 km run that is well over a hundred walls, and with a 50% spawn chance you are looking at dozens of extra ore drops that a non-Demolitionist team simply never gets. Ore feeds bars, bars sell for Coins, and Coins buy the drill upgrades that break the next wall.

It also pairs neatly with **Miner's** doubled ore and bar prices — a Demolitionist generating ore and a Miner selling it at double value is a stronger economy than either class alone. The [Miner guide](/classes/miner/) covers that side.

One detail worth knowing: **Stone-mutated enemies** and certain enemies can only be killed with pickaxes or explosives. The [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) lists exactly which ones — an explosive class is not optional in those fights.

## Half-price explosives at Level 3

Level 3 makes **explosives 50% off in Settlements**. It is a personal discount, not a team bonus, so it applies to whatever you buy for yourself — which for a Demolitionist is a lot of explosives.

Both unlock gates are cumulative damage (**100,000** then **500,000**), so they come from playing the class rather than from chasing a specific task. That makes Demolitionist one of the smoother classes to level: no armor sets to assemble, no headshot skill gate, no chest counts.

Level 4 gates at **10,000,000 m of career total depth** — the same gate [Reaper](/classes/reaper/) uses, and a long-term project rather than a weekend goal.

## Where Demolitionist fits

Demolitionist is an economy class wearing a combat class's clothes. It ranks in the A tier on our [best classes tier list](/classes/best-classes/) because it produces ore while doing something the team was going to do anyway.

It is a good second or third purchase rather than a first one — at 10,000 Cores it costs twice what [Driller](/classes/driller/) does, and Driller fixes a more urgent problem. Once your team can break walls reliably, Demolitionist turns those walls into income.

For the run where wall frequency and ore generation matter most, see [how to reach 100 KM](/guides/reach-100km/). And if you are saving the 10,000 Cores, [how to get Cores](/guides/get-cores/) lists the fastest sources.
