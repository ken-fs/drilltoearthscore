## What Healer actually does

Healer costs 3,000 Cores — tied with [Scout](/classes/scout/) as the cheapest class after the 100-Core [Miner](/classes/miner/). It is the budget support pick, and it does two things: it makes your heals bigger, and eventually it makes revives free.

You start each run with a **Medkit** and **Medic Armor**, so the class works from the moment you buy it.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 3,000 Cores | +10% Healing |
| 2 | Heal 10,000 HP | When below 20% HP, gain +10% Healing |
| 3 | Use 10 Revive Kits, heal 25,000 HP | Start each run with a Revive Kit |
| 4 | 3,000,000 m career total depth | Team bonus: +5% Healing for the whole server |

## The Revive Kit is the reason to level it

Levels 1 and 2 are percentage bumps — useful, but the kind of upgrade you forget is there. **Level 3 changes what the class is.**

Starting every run with a **Revive Kit** means reviving a downed teammate stops being a resource decision. In a 30-player co-op descent, that is the difference between a wipe and a recovery, and it is why Healer is worth levelling even if you later move to a stronger support class.

The catch is the unlock requirement: **use 10 Revive Kits**. You have to actually revive people ten times, which means you need teammates who go down — and teammates who go down are exactly what a well-played Healer prevents. On a quiet server this gate takes a while.

## Healer versus Reaper

This is the question every support player asks, and the answer is about *how* you want to heal.

| | Healer | Reaper |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | 3,000 Cores | 10,000 Cores |
| Healing style | Bigger heals, plus a free Revive Kit | Continuous healing from enemy kills |
| Level 4 gate | 3,000,000 m | 10,000,000 m |
| Best for | Coordinated teams on a budget | Solo players and long descents |

Healer gives you a bigger number when you press the button. [Reaper](/classes/reaper/) gives you healing that happens while you are doing something else — enemies have a 20% chance to drop soul orbs that heal 25 HP, and the orbs heal teammates who walk through them.

The practical split: if your team communicates and you enjoy the support role, Healer is excellent value at a third of Reaper's price. If you play with randoms or solo, Reaper's passive healing is worth the extra 7,000 Cores.

## Hitting the requirements

- **Level 2**: heal 10,000 HP total. This happens naturally if you run dungeons.
- **Level 3**: use 10 Revive Kits and heal 25,000 HP. The heal total is fine; the revive count is the gate.
- **Level 4**: 3,000,000 m of career total depth — the same gate as Scout, and one of the lowest in the game. Healer is one of the easier classes to fully finish.

## Where Healer fits

Healer is strongest in enclosed content where damage arrives in bursts: **Vampire Mansion**, **Spider Dungeons**, the **Goblin's Arena** and the three Dungeon levels. The [structures and dungeons guide](/guides/structures-and-dungeons/) maps where each spawns by layer.
