## Is Timekeeper out?

Not yet. The badge **Unlock the Timekeeper class** was created on **23 October 2026** (19:43 UTC). When we checked on 24 October, it had **zero unlocks**.

That means the class is in the game's data but players can't get it yet. It's the 31st class.

## How we know it's coming

The developers create a badge whenever they add a class. A new badge with zero unlocks means the class is staged. The first unlocks mean it's live.

Mercenary went this way: badge on 23 September, live on 26 September. Timekeeper may follow, but nothing says so yet.
