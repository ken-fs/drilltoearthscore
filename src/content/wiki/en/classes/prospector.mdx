## What Prospector actually does

Prospector is a Tier 5 Academy class at 30,000 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Prospector Armor Set, Prospector Pickaxe) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **Ore and Gem Veins drop multiple times more often (max 4)**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30,000 Cores | Ore and Gem Veins drop multiple times more often (max 4) |
| 2 | Class level requirement | Ores are automatically smelted into bars when mined |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Ores and Gem Veins always drop two or more items |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | Gems have a 5% chance to be Pristine when mined (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Prospector worth 30,000 Cores?

At Prospector's price point the question is never whether the class works - it is whether it fixes a problem you actually have. Tier 5 unlocks cost more than everything below them combined, so buy one when your runs are failing for the specific reason this class solves.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Prospector show up in multiplayer lobbies: **Gems have a 5% chance to be Pristine when mined**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.
