## Is Engineer out?

Yes, but almost nobody has it. The badge, **Unlock the Engineer class**, was created on **1 October 2026** (21:25 UTC). It's awarding unlocks, so the class is reachable in game.

The count is tiny, though. By 17 October it had **524 unlocks**, with **24** in the past day.

## Why so few players have it

Compare it with [Vampire](/classes/vampire/). Vampire's badge came six days later and passed **30,050 unlocks** in the same window. Engineer has about 1/60th of that.

We don't know the reason. It might cost a lot, or it might unlock through something other than Cores. No guide or creator video we found explains it, and we haven't found any footage of the class yet.

## What we'll add next

Once a source shows the Academy listing, we'll add the price, tier and how you unlock it. The level-by-level kit follows once someone tests it.
