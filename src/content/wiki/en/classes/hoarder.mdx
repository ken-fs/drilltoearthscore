## Is Hoarder out?

Not yet. The badge **Unlock the Hoarder class** was created on **6 November 2026** (15:20 UTC). When we checked, it had **zero unlocks**.

That means the class is in the game's data but players can't get it yet. It's the 33rd class.

## How we know it's coming

The developers create a badge whenever they add a class. A new badge with zero unlocks means the class is staged. The first unlocks mean it's live.

Other recent badges came first too. The [Timekeeper page](/classes/timekeeper/) and [Spider Queen page](/classes/spider-queen/) track what's known about those classes.
