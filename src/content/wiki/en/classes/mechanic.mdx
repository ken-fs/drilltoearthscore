## What Mechanic actually does

Mechanic costs 5,000 Cores and it solves the problem nobody talks about: **fuel**.

Everyone knows drill power ends runs. Fewer players notice that the other failure mode is a dry tank, and the game is built to punish it — the **Void Clay Wall Boss at 17,000 m** empties your drill's fuel the moment you arrive. A team that can stretch its coal further survives content that a fuel-blind team wipes on.

You start each run with a **Gas Can** and **Mechanic Armor**, and the **Magnet** arrives at Level 2.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5,000 Cores | +50% Fuel Contribution |
| 2 | Contribute 10,000 Fuel to the drill | Start each run with a Magnet that attracts loot |
| 3 | Sell 25,000 Coins worth of Scrap | Scrap sells for +100% Coins |
| 4 | 5,000,000 m career total depth | Team bonus: +25% Fuel Contribution for the whole server |

## Fuel contribution compounds across the team

**+50% fuel contribution** means every coal you feed the drill is worth half again as much. In a 30-player server that is not a personal buff — it is a team-wide resource multiplier, and it is the reason Mechanic ranks above the pure damage classes.

The practical effect shows up at the two fuel-critical moments:

- **The Void Clay Wall Boss** at 17,000 m, which drains your tank on arrival. The [100 KM plan](/guides/reach-100km/) recommends carrying 3 to 7 coal or more for exactly this fight — a Mechanic makes that coal go further.
- **Long boss fights** from the Toxic Layer down, where 25,000 HP walls and up turn fuel into a timer.

## The Magnet is a convenience that adds up

Level 2 starts you with a **Magnet** that pulls loot toward you. It sounds minor and it is not: loot scatters in structures, and the walk between drops is time you are not mining, fighting or fuelling.

In a **Mineshaft** or a **Spider Tunnel**, where ore and scrap are spread across the room, the Magnet is the difference between clearing a room and picking at it.

## Scrap value doubles at Level 3

**Scrap sells for +100% Coins** at Level 3. That makes Mechanic a genuine economy class, not just a support one.

Scrap is the most common loot in the game — the [drill upgrades guide](/items/drill-upgrades/) notes that Little and Large Walls appear every 700 m to 1 km, and structures drop scrap constantly. Doubling its sale price is a steady income floor that does not depend on finding a rare ore.

The comparison with [Miner](/classes/miner/) is worth knowing: Miner doubles **ore and bar** prices, Mechanic doubles **scrap** prices. Which is better depends on whether your runs produce more ore or more scrap — structure-heavy descents favour Mechanic.

## Hitting the requirements

- **Level 2**: contribute 10,000 Fuel to the drill
- **Level 3**: sell 25,000 Coins worth of Scrap
- **Level 4**: 5,000,000 m of career total depth

Both gates are grind tasks that the class is already built to do — you will hit 10,000 fuel just by playing the role, and 25,000 Coins of scrap follows from the doubled sale price.

## Where Mechanic fits

Mechanic is a support-economy hybrid, and it is one of the better 5,000-Core buys because it improves the team rather than adding to a damage pool that is already deep. Pair it with [Driller](/classes/driller/) for the wall and a fuel class for the tank, and the two biggest run-enders are both covered.
