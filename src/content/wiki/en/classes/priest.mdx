## What Priest actually does

Priest is a Tier 5 Academy class at 30,000 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Priest Armor Set, Priest Staff) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **Regenerate 1% of your max HP every second**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30,000 Cores | Regenerate 1% of your max HP every second |
| 2 | Class level requirement | Blessed teammates instantly revive when defeated |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Blessed teammates extend their Blessing by 5 seconds whenever they defeat an enemy |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | +50% Church spawn rate (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Priest worth 30,000 Cores?

At Priest's price point the question is never whether the class works - it is whether it fixes a problem you actually have. Tier 5 unlocks cost more than everything below them combined, so buy one when your runs are failing for the specific reason this class solves.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Priest show up in multiplayer lobbies: **+50% Church spawn rate**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.
