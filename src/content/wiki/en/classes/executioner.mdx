## What Executioner actually does

Executioner is a Tier 5 Academy class at 30,000 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Executioner Armor Set, Executioner Sword) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **+200% Damage to Bosses**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30,000 Cores | +200% Damage to Bosses |
| 2 | Class level requirement | Defeating a Boss grants a random +5% bonus: max HP, Melee, Gun, or Explosive Damage |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Bosses always drop a Gem; 60% Diamond, 30% Emerald, 10% Ruby |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | Defeating a Boss grants a free Drill Upgrade with three unique options (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Executioner worth 30,000 Cores?

At Executioner's price point the question is never whether the class works - it is whether it fixes a problem you actually have. Tier 5 unlocks cost more than everything below them combined, so buy one when your runs are failing for the specific reason this class solves.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Executioner show up in multiplayer lobbies: **Defeating a Boss grants a free Drill Upgrade with three unique options**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.
