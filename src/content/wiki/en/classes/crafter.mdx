## What Crafter actually does

Crafter is a Tier 4 Academy class at 10,000 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Crafter Armor Set, Resource Scanner, Wrench, Furnace) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **Crafting Shack prices are reduced by 10%**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10,000 Cores | Crafting Shack prices are reduced by 10% |
| 2 | Class level requirement | Shredding Scrap grants 50% of its value back in Coins |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Drill Boost grants 50% more distance, speed, and Wall Damage |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | Crafting Shack prices are reduced by 5% (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Crafter worth 10,000 Cores?

Crafter sits in the mid-tier band, which is usually the best value in the Academy: expensive enough to matter, cheap enough to reach without a dedicated grind. If its passive matches your bottleneck, it is a reasonable buy before any Tier 5.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Crafter show up in multiplayer lobbies: **Crafting Shack prices are reduced by 5%**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.

Drill Boost stacks with the upgrade cards covered in the [drill upgrades guide](/items/drill-upgrades/).
