## How classes work

Classes are lobby purchases, not in-run upgrades. You spend **Cores** in the Academy to unlock a role, then equip it before you descend. Each class hands you starter gear, a passive that scales over four levels, and a team bonus at the end.

That split matters when you are deciding what to buy. Levels 1 to 3 are personal — they make *your* job easier. **Level 4 is always a team bonus**, so it helps all 30 players on the server, and it unlocks on career total depth rather than anything you do in one run. Career depth is every meter you have ever travelled, so a 100 km clear adds 100,000 m toward the gate even if you die at the core.

You can also roll passives from classes you already own onto another class for 2,500 Cores, which is how experienced players stack bonuses from roles they do not want to main.

## What changed in Season 1

Season 1 is the live heading on the game, tagged on the Roblox page during the week of 3–8 September 2026. Two badges were created minutes apart on 3 September: **Prospector** (Unlock the Prospector class) and **Shopkeeper**, which confirms both the class and the lobby kiosk are real.

The class cadence is worth noting if you are planning a build. The developers shipped eight classes in March, one in April, one in May, two in June, five in July, four in August and two so far in September. A class you are saving for may be superseded before you afford it, so prioritise roles that fix a problem you actually have — usually drill power or survival, not raw combat damage.

## Which class to buy first

**Miner at 100 Cores is the only cheap door**, and it pays for itself because ore income funds everything else. If you have not read it yet, start with our [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) to get your first hour right.

After Miner, the honest answer depends on why your runs are failing:

- **You run out of drill power** → Driller (5,000 Cores), which also discounts drill upgrades. Pair it with the [drill upgrades guide](/items/drill-upgrades/) so your Coins go into the right cards.
- **Your team dies in dungeon rooms** → Reaper (10,000 Cores) or Tank (5,000 Cores). Our [Reaper guide](/classes/reaper/) covers the soul orb loop in detail.
- **You are poor, not weak** → Lucky Looter (10,000 Cores) is the main economy buy, and [earning Cores](/guides/get-cores/) is the fastest fix.
