## What Salvager actually does

Salvager is a Tier 4 Academy class at 10,000 Cores. Like every class in the game, it hands you starter gear (Salvager Armor Set) and then scales through four levels - three personal upgrades and a team bonus at the end.

The Level 1 passive is the identity: **Enemies have a 10% chance to drop iron bars**. Everything after that builds on the same idea rather than switching lanes.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 10,000 Cores | Enemies have a 10% chance to drop iron bars |
| 2 | Class level requirement | Shredding Scrap grants 50% more Cogs |
| 3 | Class level requirement | Scrap can be sold for 100% extra Coins |
| 4 | Career total depth gate | Enemies have a 5% chance to drop iron bars (team bonus) |

Level 4 is the team bonus - it applies to the whole 30-player server and gates on career total depth, not on anything you do in one run.

## Is Salvager worth 10,000 Cores?

Salvager sits in the mid-tier band, which is usually the best value in the Academy: expensive enough to matter, cheap enough to reach without a dedicated grind. If its passive matches your bottleneck, it is a reasonable buy before any Tier 5.

## Where it fits in a team

The team bonus is what makes Salvager show up in multiplayer lobbies: **Enemies have a 5% chance to drop iron bars**. In a 30-player server that is a bonus nobody has to spend their own Cores on, which is why team-first classes get picked even by players who main something else.
