## What Miner actually does

Miner is the 100-Core class, and it is the only cheap unlock in Drill to Earth's Core. Everything else starts at 3,000 Cores. That price gap tells you what the developers intended: you buy Miner first, and its pickaxe income pays for whatever comes next.

You start each run with a **Rusty Pickaxe** and **Miner Armor**, so you are not empty-handed at depth zero.

If you have not spent anything yet, the [beginner guide](/guides/beginner-guide/) walks through your first hour, and the [all classes page](/classes/all-classes/) has every price in one table.

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 Cores | +50% Pickaxe Damage |
| 2 | Mine 100 Coal, Mine 25 Diamonds | 25% chance to deal +300% Pickaxe Damage |
| 3 | Smelt 50 Iron Ore, Smelt 25 Gold Ore | Ores and Bars sell for +100% Coins |
| 4 | 500,000 m career total depth | Team bonus: +25% Pickaxe Damage for the whole server |

The Level 2 proc is the one that changes how mining feels. A flat +50% is nice; a one-in-four chance to hit for quadruple damage is what actually shortens the time you spend swinging at an ore node. In practice it means diamond and ruby nodes — the slow ones — come down noticeably faster.

## Hitting the requirements

The Level 2 gate is **100 Coal and 25 Diamonds**. Coal appears from the Grass Layer down and you will hit 100 without trying. Diamonds are the real requirement, and they only start showing up deeper, so Level 2 is a depth check disguised as a mining task.

Level 3 is different: it needs **50 Iron Ore and 25 Gold Ore smelted**. Smelting happens at a **Furnace Post**, which has roughly a 10% chance to spawn as a structure next to the drill. That randomness is why Level 3 takes longer than the numbers suggest — you need the furnace, not just the ore.

Level 4 gates on **500,000 m of career total depth**, the lowest gate in the game. Miner is the easiest class to finish, which is another reason it is the right first buy.

## Where Miner fits

Miner is an income class, not a combat one. Two things make it worth returning to after you have better classes:

- **Level 3 doubles ore and bar sale prices.** On a dedicated mining run that beats most damage passives for value.
- **It stacks with other economy picks.** Pair it with the [Mechanic](/classes/mechanic/) for scrap value, and read [how to earn Cores](/guides/get-cores/) if you are saving toward a 10,000-Core class.

If your runs are ending because the drill cannot break walls rather than because you are poor, [Driller](/classes/driller/) is the better 5,000-Core answer. The [best classes tier list](/classes/best-classes/) ranks the whole roster by what each one fixes.
