## What Gunslinger actually does

Gunslinger costs 5,000 Cores and it is the ranged damage class. You start each run with a **Revolver** and **Gunslinger Armor** — a real weapon, not a starter placeholder, so the class is useful immediately.

Its three levels stack in a way the melee classes do not: a flat bonus, then a skill-based multiplier, then a raw rate-of-fire doubling. That last one is why Gunslinger sits above the other damage classes in our [tier list](/classes/best-classes/).

## Level by level

| Level | Unlock | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5,000 Cores | +50% Gun Damage |
| 2 | Defeat 1,000 enemies with guns, deal 1,000 damage in one shot | Headshots give +50% Gun Damage |
| 3 | Land 3,000 headshots, deal 3,000 damage in one shot | Guns fire +100% faster |
| 4 | 5,000,000 m career total depth | Team bonus: +25% Gun Damage for the whole server |

## Fire rate doubling is the payoff

**Level 3 doubles how fast your guns fire.** That is not a percentage bonus on top of a percentage bonus — it is twice as many shots in the same window, and every one of them carries the Level 1 and Level 2 multipliers.

The maths compounds. A gun doing +50% base damage, with headshots adding another +50%, fired twice as often, is roughly a 4.5x throughput increase over a stock weapon. No other class in the game gets a single upgrade that large.

The cost is the requirement: **3,000 headshots**. That is a real skill gate, and it is why Gunslinger takes longer to finish than Bruiser even though both cost 5,000 Cores.

## Headshots make it a precision class

Level 2 rewards aiming: **headshots give +50% gun damage**. Stacked on the Level 1 bonus, a headshot from a levelled Gunslinger does more than double the damage of a body shot from a stock gun.

That has a practical consequence in deeper layers. From the Crystal Layer down, the enemy list adds **Bats** at 27 speed and **Fuel Leeches** at 53 speed — the fastest enemies in the game, and both hard to hit. Gunslinger's fire rate helps, but the class rewards players who can actually track a moving target.

It also keeps you at range, which matters against the **Watcher**. The Watcher deals instant-kill damage at 25 speed and cannot be killed with normal weapons — the [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) lists which enemies ignore standard damage.

## Hitting the requirements

- **Level 2**: 1,000 gun kills, 1,000 damage in one shot
- **Level 3**: 3,000 headshots, 3,000 damage in one shot
- **Level 4**: 5,000,000 m of career total depth

The single-shot damage numbers gate on weapon quality. Better guns come from structures — **Vampire Mansion** drops Golden Keys that open gear chests, and **Goblin Champion** drops a full equipment set. The [structures and dungeons guide](/guides/structures-and-dungeons/) maps where each spawns.

## Where Gunslinger fits

Gunslinger is a damage class, and damage is the least scarce resource in a 30-player server. That does not make it bad — it makes it a *later* purchase. Buy it once your team has [Driller](/classes/driller/) for the wall and something for survival, then enjoy having the highest personal DPS on the run.
