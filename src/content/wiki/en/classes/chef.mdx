## Is Chef out?

It looks like it. The badge **Unlock the Chef class** was created on **20 November 2026** (17:32 UTC). It had zero unlocks on 21 November and **9 unlocks** on 22 November.

That means players can get the class now. It's the 34th class.

Only the first two rows are solid. We leave the rest blank on purpose, because a made-up price would send you grinding toward a number that doesn't exist.

## How we know it's coming

The developers create a badge whenever they add a class. A new badge with zero unlocks means the class is staged. The first unlocks mean it's live, and that's what happened here.

The [Hoarder page](/classes/hoarder/) shows the same pattern from earlier in November.
