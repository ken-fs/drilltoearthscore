## Is Chef out?

Not yet. The badge **Unlock the Chef class** was created on **20 November 2026** (17:32 UTC). When we checked on 21 November, it had **zero unlocks**.

That means the class is in the game's data but players can't get it yet. It's the 34th class.

## How we know it's coming

The developers create a badge whenever they add a class. A new badge with zero unlocks means the class is staged. The first unlocks mean it's live.

The [Hoarder page](/classes/hoarder/) shows the same pattern from earlier in November.
