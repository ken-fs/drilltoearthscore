## How structures work

Structures spawn next to the drill and you explore them for loot and equipment. Their difficulty is deliberately uneven: some are safe scrap rooms, others will end your run.

Two spawn rules matter:

- **The first Dungeon is guaranteed at 3 km.**
- **After that, a structure spawns roughly every 7 km**, randomly chosen from the pool available at your depth.

So a run is not a straight line — it is a series of decisions about which rooms are worth the risk at your current gear level.

## Main structures

| Structure | Enemies | Loot | Difficulty | Spawns in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dungeon Level 1 | Goblin, Goblin Brute | All Scrap, Chest | 🟩 | Grass Layer |
| Spider Tunnel | Spider | Chests | 🟩 | Dirt Layer |
| Mineshaft | Watcher, Spider | Ore, Gem | 🟨🟨 | Dirt Layer |
| Dungeon Level 2 | Goblin, Goblin Brute, Spider, Goblin King | All Scrap, Chests | 🟨🟨 | Stone Layer |
| Dungeon Level 3 | Goblin, Goblin Brute, Spider, Mole Man | All Scrap, Chests | 🟧🟧🟧 | Stone Layer |
| Vampire Mansion | Vampire | Golden Keys, Chests | 🟥🟥🟥🟥 | Clay Layer |
| Spider Dungeons | Spider, Venom Spider, Red Widow | Chests | 🟥🟥🟥🟥 | Crystal Layer |
| Goblin's Arena | Goblin, Goblin Brute, Goblin Knight, Goblin Champion | Chests | 🟥🟥🟥🟥🟥 | Crystal Layer |

The layer names map to depth ranges on the [layers page](/guides/layers-and-depths/): Grass is 0–10,000 m, Dirt 10,000–17,000 m, Stone follows, and Clay sits at 17,000–24,000 m. Crystal is 31,000–41,000 m.

### The Mineshaft is a trap

Every structure above has a difficulty rating that roughly matches its contents — except the **Mineshaft**.

It is rated 🟨🟨, the same as Dungeon Level 2, but it contains **Watchers**. A Watcher has 500 HP, moves at 25 speed, deals **instant-kill** damage, and cannot be killed with normal weapons. It is also a mineshaft, so you are fighting in a confined space with ore scattered around you.

Treat a Dirt Layer Mineshaft as a four-square room regardless of its label. The [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) explains which enemies ignore standard weapons.

### The Goblin's Arena is the loot room

Five squares of difficulty, and worth it. The Arena contains Goblins, Goblin Brutes, Goblin Knights and **Goblin Champions** — and the Champion drops a full equipment set (Champion's Helmet, Chest, Legs and Hammer).

If you need gear rather than Coins, this is the room. A [Goblin King](/classes/goblin-king/) class build gets extra value here, since goblins sell for double.

### The Vampire Mansion gates your gear

Vampires drop **Golden Keys** and **Gold Bars**, and the mansion's chests are what those keys open. At 🟥🟥🟥🟥 it is the hardest Clay Layer content, and Clay is where the **Void Clay Wall Boss** waits — the wall that empties your fuel. If you are going to run the mansion, do it before the boss wall, not after.

## Other structures

| Structure | Chance | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drill Crash | Loot room — 3 items, Goblin | Free early loot |
| Furnace Post | 10% | Smelts Iron and Gold ore into bars |
| Church | Every 300 km | Saves items and player equipment |

**Furnace Post** is easy to overlook and it gates a class upgrade: [Miner](/classes/miner/) Level 3 requires smelting 50 Iron Ore and 25 Gold Ore, and smelting only happens at a Furnace Post. A 10% spawn chance is why that upgrade takes longer than the ore count suggests.

**Church** spawns on a fixed schedule rather than a random roll — once every 300 km. It saves your items and equipment, which makes it a checkpoint. On a 100 km run you will not see one; on a long multi-hour descent, the Church is what stops a wipe from costing you everything.

**Drill Crash** is the freebie: three items and a single Goblin, usually early in a run.

## What to run and when

A rough priority order that follows the difficulty curve:

1. **Dungeon Level 1 (Grass)** — always worth it. Scrap and a Chest, low risk.
2. **Spider Tunnel (Dirt)** — easy Chests. Spiders are 100 HP and slow to threaten a group.
3. **Mineshaft (Dirt)** — only with explosives or pickaxes, or a very coordinated group. Otherwise skip.
4. **Dungeon Levels 2 and 3 (Stone)** — Scrap and Chests with escalating enemies. Level 3 adds Mole Men at 2,000 HP and 30 damage.
5. **Vampire Mansion (Clay)** — go for Golden Keys, before the Clay boss wall.
6. **Spider Dungeons and Goblin's Arena (Crystal)** — the endgame loot rooms. The Arena is the better reward per risk.

Chest tier determines the Core payout — **Wooden 1x, Golden 2x, Diamond 5x, Void 10x** — so deeper structures pay disproportionately better. The [Cores guide](/guides/get-cores/) covers the multiplier maths, and [how to reach 100 KM](/guides/reach-100km/) is the run plan that sequences all of this.

If you are picking a class to run structures with, [Tank](/classes/tank/) redirects damage in the enclosed rooms and [Healer](/classes/healer/) keeps the party up — the [best classes tier list](/classes/best-classes/) compares both.
