## Every armor tier

Armor in Drill to Earth's Core comes in seven tiers, and each one has a matching badge awarded for wearing the complete set. The badges are how the tiers are confirmed: the developers create one the moment a set ships, so the badge list is the authoritative record.

| Tier | Full-set badge | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rusty | Rusty Armor | The starter set. Tank begins with a Rusty Chest |
| Steel | Steel Armor | Added March 2026 |
| Gold | Gold Armor | Added March 2026 |
| Diamond | Diamond Armor | Mid-game set; gates Tank Level 2 |
| Void | Void Armor | Later set; gates Tank Level 3 |
| Phoenix | Phoenix Armor | Added June 2026 |
| King's | King's Armor | Added August 2026 |

Each set awards its badge the moment you equip all four pieces, which means the armor badges are effectively a progression checklist. You can see the full badge list in the [Cores guide](/guides/get-cores/), since achievements are also a Core source.

## Armor is a class gate, not just stats

The reason to care about armor tiers is that **Tank gates on them**:

- **[Tank](/classes/tank/) Level 2** requires tanking 10,000 damage **and wearing a full Diamond Armor set**
- **[Tank](/classes/tank/) Level 3** requires tanking 25,000 damage **and wearing a full Void Armor set**

Both halves matter. Tanking the damage happens naturally if you play the class; assembling four matching pieces means finding them first. That is why Tank takes noticeably longer to level than its 5,000-Core price suggests.

No other class gates on armor. The rest use kill counts, damage totals, chest counts or fuel contribution — the [best classes tier list](/classes/best-classes/) summarises what each one needs.

## Where armor comes from

Armor pieces drop from **structures and chests**, and a few enemies drop equipment directly:

| Source | What it gives |
| --- | --- |
| Goblin Champion | Champion's Helmet, Chest, Legs, Hammer |
| Goblin Knight | Steel Sword |
| Gunner Miner | Crazy Miner Chest, Helmet |
| Vampire | Golden Keys — which open gear chests in the Vampire Mansion |
| Structures and chests | Armor pieces by tier, deeper layers give better tiers |

The **Goblin Champion** is the most efficient single source: it drops a complete set from one enemy type, and it appears in the **Goblin's Arena** in the Crystal Layer. The [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) has the drop table, and the [structures and dungeons guide](/guides/structures-and-dungeons/) maps where each structure spawns.

**Golden Keys** are the other route. Vampires drop them, and they open the chests inside Vampire Mansions — which is the Clay Layer's dedicated gear room. Since Clay is also where the Void Clay Wall Boss waits, the sensible order is mansion first, boss wall second.

## Armor and the rest of your build

Armor is one of three gear systems, and they solve different problems:

- **Armor** — survivability, and the Tank upgrade gate
- **Weapons** — damage, and the gate for Gunslinger and Bruiser single-hit requirements
- **[Drill upgrades](/items/drill-upgrades/)** — the wall-breaking system, bought with Coins during a run

The drill upgrade system is the one that actually ends runs. Boss wall HP climbs from **100 in the Grass Layer to 500,000 before the 100 km mark** and 5,000,000 at the Heart of the Earth, and no amount of personal armor or weapon damage keeps pace with that. The [layers page](/guides/layers-and-depths/) has the full HP table.

So: collect armor for the badges and for Tank's gates, but spend your Coins on **RPM Overclock, Ultra Drill and Power Burst** — the three cards that break walls. The [drill upgrades guide](/items/drill-upgrades/) explains why that trio works as a set.
