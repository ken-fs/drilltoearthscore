## Every ore and what it pays

Mining ore is the income loop at the centre of Drill to Earth's Core. Each ore type has a matching achievement, and those achievements pay **Cores** — the lobby currency that buys classes.

| Ore | Achievement reward | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coal | 5 Cores | Grass Layer and everywhere below |
| Iron | 30 Cores | From 3,000 m |
| Gold | 45 Cores | From 3,000 m |
| Diamond | 60 Cores | Deeper layers |
| Ruby | **175 Cores** | Deeper layers |
| Emerald | Not confirmed | Deepest layers |
| Heartgem | Not confirmed | Endgame layers |

**Ruby is the standout.** At 175 Cores it is the largest single achievement reward in the game — more than Diamond, Gold and Iron combined. If you are saving toward a 5,000-Core class, Ruby nodes are the fastest honest route.

**Coal is the workhorse.** It pays almost nothing as an achievement, but coal is the fuel ore: you feed it to the drill, and running dry is one of the two main ways runs fail. The [100 KM plan](/guides/reach-100km/) recommends carrying 3 to 7 coal or more specifically for the Void Clay Wall Boss at 17,000 m, which empties your tank on arrival.

## Ores as achievements

The ore achievements sit inside a larger badge list, and the cheap ones are worth grabbing early:

| Badge | Requirement | Cores |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mine Ruby | Break a ruby ore node | 175 |
| Mine Diamond | Break a diamond ore node | 60 |
| Mine Gold | Break a gold ore node | 45 |
| Mine Iron | Break an iron ore node | 30 |
| Mine Coal | Break a coal ore node | 5 |

The [Cores guide](/guides/get-cores/) has the full achievement table alongside chest multipliers and boss wall payouts, which is where the real Core income comes from.

## Smelting: ore into bars

**Iron and Gold ore can be smelted into bars** at a **Furnace Post**. The Furnace Post has roughly a **10% chance** to spawn as a structure next to the drill, which makes it the rarest structure in the game by a wide margin.

Bars are worth more Coins than raw ore. But the bigger reason to care is that smelting gates a class upgrade:

**[Miner](/classes/miner/) Level 3 requires smelting 50 Iron Ore and 25 Gold Ore** and unlocks doubled ore and bar sale prices. Without a Furnace Post you cannot finish that upgrade — which is why a class that looks like a quick grind turns into a multi-run project.

The practical advice: when a Furnace Post does spawn, use it. Do not walk past it planning to smelt later, because the 10% roll does not come around often.

## Making ore pay

Three things multiply what a mining run is worth:

**Miner class** — Level 3 doubles ore and bar sale prices. It is the single biggest ore-income upgrade in the game and it costs only 100 Cores to unlock the class. The [Miner guide](/classes/miner/) covers the level gates.

**Demolitionist class** — Level 2 gives walls broken by explosives a 50% chance to spawn ores. Since Little and Large Walls appear every 700 m to 1 km, that converts the game's most repetitive activity into an ore source. See the [Demolitionist guide](/classes/demolitionist/).

**Lucky Looter class** — the chest and ore multiplier class at 10,000 Cores. It is the strongest economy pick in the game, and the [best classes tier list](/classes/best-classes/) ranks it in the top tier.

Mined ore also does not exist in a vacuum — it feeds bars, bars feed Coins, and Coins buy the [drill upgrades](/items/drill-upgrades/) that break the next wall. If your runs are ending at the wall rather than at the ore node, the bottleneck is drill power, not mining speed.

## Where to find ore

Ore appears in **structures** — Mineshafts, Dungeons and the rooms beside the drill — and from **Stone-mutated enemies**, which explode into random ores when killed. The [structures and dungeons guide](/guides/structures-and-dungeons/) maps which rooms hold ore by layer, and the [enemies guide](/guides/enemies-and-mutations/) explains the Stone mutation.

Deeper layers hold rarer ore, so the honest answer to "where do I find Ruby" is: go deeper. The [layers page](/guides/layers-and-depths/) has every depth range and what changes at each one.
